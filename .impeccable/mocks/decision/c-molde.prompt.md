A mobile web page, phone viewport in portrait, rendered edge to edge with crisp readable Spanish text, no device frame and no browser chrome. It is "Norma · cerámica", the one-page catalog of Norma, a 70-year-old ceramicist and retired art teacher from Quilmes, Argentina. Visitors are people around 70 looking at her pieces on their phone: they want to see each piece, its price, and how to ask for it. There is no cart; they message her on WhatsApp and tell her the piece number.

The page is built as a home sewing pattern envelope, like the Burda and magazine patterns Argentine women sewed from. The front shows her pieces as real photographs labelled with large circled piece numbers, with one featured piece traced by a dressmaker-red dashed outline. Below that, the envelope's back table lists every piece in big type, with number, name and price: "14 · Jarra blanca · $ 12.000", "7 · Taza celeste · $ 8.500", "23 · Ensaladera grande · $ 15.000", "9 · Florero alto · $ 11.000". At the top: "Norma · cerámica" and "Hola, soy Norma. Estas son las piezas que tengo ahora en el taller." At the bottom, starting the next section: "¿Te gustó alguna? Escribime al 11 2345-6789 y contame cuál. Cada pieza tiene su numerito, así no nos confundimos."

The attached images are style inspiration for this world, adapted into a real working mobile page; do not copy their layout or content. Very large, high-contrast type for readers of 70. No navigation, no cart, no buttons.
